# Multi-Perk Mod for Call of Duty: Black Ops 2 Zombies
![Multi-Perks-Preview](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/355a7126-ed1d-4b80-803a-e0760305f050)

## Overview
This mod enhances the classic Call of Duty: Black Ops 2 Zombies experience by introducing **Multi-Perk Scaling**, a feature that scales the price and effects of the vanilla perks dynamically. As players stack perks, their effects grow in power, but so does the price, providing a fresh strategic layer to the gameplay and **Custom Perks** with their unique mechanics to let the player create new plans to survive.

### Key Features
- **Scaling Perks**: Vanilla perks (Juggernog, Mule Kick, etc.) have effects and prices that scale as more perks are added.
- **Increased Replayability**: Encourages unique strategies based on scaling perks and dynamic pricing.

## **Custom Perks**:
- **Muscle Milk**: Electric Melee attacks. Available at the Juggernog machine.
- **Point Crusher**: Scaling Points multiplier (also boosts Vulture Aid bonus points drops). Available at the Mule Kick machine.
- **Multi-Speed Cola**: Incremental reload speed booster (+20% reload speed per upgrade level) bought by pressing Melee button near the Speed Cola machine.
- **Exo Suit**: Advanced Exo mechanics — Double Jump, Air Dash Boost, visceral High-Velocity Ground Slam (with drop-height damage/radius scaling, lethal direct-impact crush stomp, and controller rumble), mid-air Hover, and **Exo Slide** (run + press Change Stance to slide with crouch lock, 430->190 speed curve, thrusters and dirt friction sound, dust and spark FX, controller slide rumble, and jump-cancel into double jump). Slam damage scales further with Dam-A-Nade! Available at the Stamin-Up machine.
- **Multi-Stamin-Up**: Incremental movement speed booster (+10% walk and run speed per upgrade level) bought by pressing Melee button near the Stamin-Up machine.
- **Bandolier Bandit**: Increases maximum reserve ammo capacity by +20% per upgrade level. Dynamically scales Max Ammo pickups, weapon wall-buys, mystery box weapons, console `give ammo`, and Vulture Aid ammo drops. Costs 3,000 points (+3,000 per level). Available at the Stamin-Up machine (Aim `[{+speed_throw}]` menu).
- **Blood Bullets**: Ammo in your active gun slowly regenerates directly into the clip over time, with frequency dynamically scaled by weapon clip size. Costs 3,000 points. Available at the Stamin-Up machine (Aim `[{+speed_throw}]` menu).
- **Nitrogen Cooled**: Nearby zombies within radius are temporarily cooled and slowed to a walking pace, regaining normal speed once leaving range or upon player down. Costs 2,000 points. Available at the Stamin-Up machine (`[{+breath_sprint}]`).
- **Punch-a-Cola**: Scaling bullet damage multiplier — Pack-a-Punch yourself! Available at the Double Tap machine.
- **Vigor Rush**: Bullet weapons create small explosive impact bursts when shooting zombies, dealing extra direct and splash damage. Costs 3,000 points. Available at the Double Tap machine (Aim `[{+speed_throw}]` menu).
- **Multi-Double Tap 1.0**: Incremental fire rate booster (+15% fire rate per upgrade level) bought by holding Aim near the Double Tap machine.
- **Vulture Aid**: Killed zombies drop ammo packs, bonus points, or a green stink cloud that makes zombies ignore you while standing inside. Available at the Juggernog machine.
- **Winter's Wail**: Taking damage from zombies while wounded unleashes a frost blast that freezes nearby zombies solid before thawing them slow. Holds up to 3 charges that recharge over time and refill each round. Available at the Double Tap machine.
- **Cashback Cocktail**: Get 20% cash back on any purchase, with an upgrade to 30% cash back (+10% more). Available at the Speed Cola machine (Aim `[{+speed_throw}]` menu).
- **Ethereal Razor**: Melee cleaves multiple nearby zombies, massively increases melee damage (+x5 damage multiplier per upgrade level), and melee hits heal the player. Works with all melee weapons, knife upgrades, Bowie knife, Galvaknuckles, spoons, upgraded staffs, and all One Inch Punch variants, plus ballistic knife projectile damage boost. Available at the Juggernog machine (Aim `[{+speed_throw}]` menu).
- **Fighter's Fizz**: Keep your primary weapons when downed and cycle weapons by pressing Melee `[{+melee}]`, Interact `[{+activate}]`, or Sprint `[{+breath_sprint}]`! Score a kill while downed to self-revive and retain all vanilla and modded perks. Seamlessly integrates with Who's Who and solo Quick Revive without soft-locking solo lobbies. Available at the Quick Revive machine (Aim `[{+speed_throw}]` menu).
- **Repairman Rum**: Rebuild window barriers super fast and gain x4 repair points (+x4 points multiplier per upgrade level). Ripping boards detonate explosive bursts. Available at the Speed Cola machine (Aim `[{+speed_throw}]` menu).
- **Dam-A-Nade**: Stronger explosives (x2 for RPG/Ray Gun/launchers and Exo Suit slam, x10 for grenades per upgrade level), carry +1 lethal & +1 tactical grenade, and regenerate all lethal grenades plus 1 tactical at round end. Available at the Juggernog machine (Aim `[{+speed_throw}]` menu).
- **Double Tap Machine in Origins**: A custom Double Tap machine located near Generator 2 that allows players to use Punch-a-Cola, Winter's Wail, Vigor Rush, and Multi-Double Tap 1.0.
- **Stamin-Up Machine in Mob of the Dead**: A custom Stamin-Up machine located at the Spiral Staircase that allows players to use the Exo Suit, Multi-Stamin-Up, Bandolier Bandit, Blood Bullets, and Nitrogen Cooled.

### Installation
1. Download the compiled file from [here](https://github.com/ReFraX32/BO2-Zombies-Multi-Perks/releases)
2. Press Windows + R then type `%localappdata%` and press Enter.
3. Place the compiled mod (`multi_perks-compiled.gsc`) in the `Plutonium\storage\t6\scripts\zm` folder.
4. Start a Zombies game and enjoy the enhanced perks system and custom perks!
