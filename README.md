# Multi-Perk Mod for Call of Duty: Black Ops 2 Zombies
![Multi-Perks-Preview](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/355a7126-ed1d-4b80-803a-e0760305f050)

## Overview
This mod enhances the classic Call of Duty: Black Ops 2 Zombies experience by introducing **Multi-Perk Scaling**, a feature that scales the price and effects of the vanilla perks dynamically. As players stack perks, their effects grow in power, but so does the price, providing a fresh strategic layer to the gameplay and **Custom Perks** with their unique mechanics to let the player create new plans to survive.

### Key Features
- **Scaling Perks**: Vanilla perks (Juggernog, Mule Kick, etc.) have effects and prices that scale as more perks are added.
- **Increased Replayability**: Encourages unique strategies based on scaling perks and dynamic pricing.

## **Custom Perks by Perk Machine**

### Quick Revive Machine
1. **Muscle Milk**: Electric melee attacks that shock and eliminate zombies on knife hits. Activated by holding Interact near the Quick Revive machine.
2. **Who's Who**: The iconic revival ghost perk ported to all maps. When downed, spawn as a phantom clone to revive yourself and keep all weapons, perks, and mod upgrades. Activated by pressing Melee.
3. **Repairman Rum**: Rebuild window barricades super fast, gain x4 repair points (+x4 points multiplier per upgrade level), and turn zombie barricade rips into explosive board traps. Activated by pressing Sprint.
4. **Fighter's Fizz**: Keep your primary weapons in last stand and switch weapons with Melee, Interact, or Sprint. Scoring a kill while downed instantly self-revives you, keeping all perks. Seamlessly integrates with Who's Who and solo Quick Revive. Activated via the Aim menu (Aim + Hold Interact).
5. **No Bear Brew**: Prevents teddy bears from the Mystery Box and bear bottles from Der Wunderfizz. Upgrade allows the player to move the Mystery Box or Der Wunderfizz to any inactive location once per round by approaching it and pressing Interact. Activated via the Aim menu (Aim + Press Melee).
6. **Pack-A-Box**: Grants a scaling chance (+20% per upgrade level, up to 100% guarantee) to receive an already Pack-A-Punched upgraded weapon directly from the Mystery Box with randomized visible optic sights. Activated via the Aim menu (Aim + Press Sprint).

### Juggernog Machine
1. **Multi-Juggernog**: Scaling health booster (+150 HP per upgrade level) that increases your maximum survivability against zombie hordes.
2. **Vulture Aid**: Ported from Buried to all maps. Killed zombies drop ammo pouches, bonus points, or a green stink cloud that camouflages you, making zombies ignore you while inside.
3. **Timeslip**: Accelerates Mystery Box weapon cycling, Der Wunderfizz bottle cycling, and speeds up Pack-A-Punch insert, upgrade process, and retrieve speeds. Upgrade makes Mystery Box cycling instant! Activated by pressing Sprint.
4. **Dam-A-Nade**: Stronger explosives (x2 for Ray Gun/launchers/Exo Slam, x10 for grenades per upgrade level), carry +1 lethal and +1 tactical grenade, and automatically regenerate all lethal grenades plus 1 tactical grenade every round end. Activated via the Aim menu.
5. **Ethereal Razor**: Melee attacks cleave through multiple nearby zombies, deal massive damage (+x5 damage multiplier per upgrade level), and melee hits heal the player. Fully compatible with all knife upgrades, Bowie Knife, Galvaknuckles, upgraded staffs, One Inch Punch variants, and ballistic knives. Activated via the Aim menu.
6. **Guardian Angel Ale**: Taking damage activates 5 seconds of invincibility, requiring 25 zombie kills to recharge. Upgrade grants a 0.6-second invincibility buffer when taking a hit while the perk is recharging. Activated via the Aim menu (Aim + Press Sprint).

### Speed Cola Machine
1. **Multi-Speed Cola**: Incremental reload speed booster (+20% reload speed per upgrade level) bought by pressing Melee near the Speed Cola machine.
2. **Point Crusher**: Scaling points multiplier (+x1 multiplier per upgrade level) for all zombie kills, board repairs, and boosts Vulture Aid bonus points drops. Activated by holding Interact.
3. **Cashback Cocktail**: Get 20% cash back on any in-game purchase, with an upgrade to 30% cash back (+10% more). Activated via the Aim menu.
4. **Tactiquilla Sangria**: Fire and reload while sprinting without losing sprint momentum, gain a 20% chance to regain fired ammunition, and earn bonus points from multi-kills (+10 points per zombie killed quickly). Upgrade increases ammo refund chance to 40% and doubles multi-kill bonus points. Activated via the Aim menu (Aim + Press Melee).

### Stamin-Up Machine
1. **Multi-Stamin-Up**: Incremental movement speed booster (+10% walk and sprint speed per upgrade level) that also grants unlimited sprint from Level 1, bought by pressing Melee near the Stamin-Up machine.
2. **Exo Suit**: Advanced Exo movement suite: Double Jump, Air Dash Boost, mid-air Hover, Ground Slam (damage scales further with Dam-A-Nade), and **Exo Slide** (sprint + Change Stance to slide; triggers an explosive shockwave on zombie impact with a 5-second cooldown, and slide speed dynamically scales with Multi-Stamin-Up!). Activated by holding Interact.
3. **Nitrogen Cooled**: Nearby zombies within radius are chilled and slowed to a walking pace, regaining normal speed once leaving range or upon player down. Activated by pressing Sprint.
4. **Bandolier Bandit**: Increases maximum reserve ammo capacity by +20% per upgrade level, with an intelligent native ammo stash system that dynamically scales Max Ammo pickups, wall-buys, and Vulture Aid ammo drops. Activated via the Aim menu.
5. **Blood Bullets**: Ammo slowly regenerates directly into your active weapon magazine over time, with frequency dynamically scaled by weapon clip size. Activated via the Aim menu.

### Double Tap Machine
1. **Multi-Double Tap 1.0**: Incremental fire rate booster (+15% fire rate per upgrade level) bought by holding Aim near the Double Tap machine.
2. **Punch-a-Cola**: Scaling bullet damage multiplier (+25% bullet damage per upgrade level) — Pack-a-Punch yourself! Activated by holding Interact.
3. **Winter's Wail**: Defensive frost blast (BO4 recreation) triggered when taking zombie damage while below full health; freezes nearby zombies solid for 5 seconds before thawing them slow. Holds 3 charges that recharge over time, refill each round, and refill with Max Ammo. Activated via the Aim menu.
4. **Vigor Rush**: High-frequency explosive impact bursts on bullet hits when shooting zombies, dealing direct and splash damage to crowds even with rapid-fire weapons. Activated via the Aim menu.

### Mule Kick Machine
1. **Multi-Mule Kick**: Scaling primary weapon capacity booster allowing players to purchase and carry extra primary weapons.

## **Custom Map Perk Machines**
- **Double Tap Machine on Origins**: Located near Generator 2. Synchronized with the Generator 2 power grid: functions when Generator 2 is activated, and shuts down if Generator 2 is unpowered.
- **Stamin-Up Machine on Mob of the Dead**: Custom Stamin-Up machine using the Double Tap Root Beer machine located at the Spiral Staircase, enabling the Exo Suit, Multi-Stamin-Up, Bandolier Bandit, Blood Bullets, and Nitrogen Cooled on Mob of the Dead.
- **Quick Revive Machine on Mob of the Dead**: Custom Quick Revive machine located near the spawn using the Speed Cola machine. Brings Quick Revive and its entire suite of custom perks (Muscle Milk, Who's Who, Repairman Rum, Fighter's Fizz, No Bear Brew, Pack-A-Box) to Mob of the Dead.

### Installation
1. Download the compiled file from [here](https://github.com/ReFraX32/BO2-Zombies-Multi-Perks/releases)
2. Press Windows + R then type `%localappdata%` and press Enter.
3. Place the compiled mod (`multi_perks-compiled.gsc`) in the `Plutonium\storage\t6\scripts\zm` folder.
